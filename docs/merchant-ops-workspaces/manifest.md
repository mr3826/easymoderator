## Initial research and decisions

These decisions incorporate the completed read-only contract, commercial, security, and pre-implementation QA reviews; they do not authorize unsupported features.

1. **Orders work surface:** Replace passive overview boxes with first-screen search, supported filters, and a real worklist. Keep any counts only if they are backed by the same authoritative query and navigate to the exact matching records. The UX audit found current card counts are computed from the currently fetched, date/status-filtered array, capped at 100; do not present them as whole-queue totals.
2. **Customers work surface:** Prioritize lookup and verified customer context. A wide desktop split may show the list and a selected customer's context together; mobile remains list-to-detail. Do not make the RTO network explanation/configuration a permanent oversized panel. Keep risk evidence contextual and explanatory.
3. **Brand authority:** The V6 persona context pack (`manifest.json`, created 2026-10-20) and `personas/03_BrandForge_Brand_Identity_Guardian.md:8-10` record the founder's later explicit selection of Bold & Future-Facing: midnight `#17223B`, violet `#7460D9`, sunlight `#F4C35E`, light surface `#F7F8FC`. The current user directly requests applying the new brand in the app. This supersedes the earlier 2026-10-15 V5 audit's provisional palette and the older design-system draft's unapproved status. Inspect actual assets, centralize semantic tokens and validate contrast before rollout. No replacement logo artwork is present in the app; retain the existing mark/wordmark geometry and make all usage consistent rather than inventing a logo.
4. **Localization:** Bangla and English copy may be idiomatic rather than literal translations, but actions, permissions, amounts, risk meaning, state and failure/recovery semantics must remain equivalent. Avoid hype in operational CTAs.
5. **Implementation ownership:** Parallelize read-only reviews. Serialize writes in one mission tree: Orders, verify; Customers, verify; then shared brand/type polish, verify. Independent QA and release review happen after a frozen candidate SHA.
6. **Methods:** Source/API/state tracing; merchant job and error-recovery mapping; independent value/security challenge; task-based prototype/browser tests; cross-viewport, locale and zoom checks; exact-artifact release verification. Merchant interview/time-on-task evidence is not yet available and must not be fabricated.
7. **Persona provenance:** Loaded the V6 CTO, BazarLens, BrandForge, merchant psychology, SaaS UX architect and independent QA/security contracts from `D:\easymod\EasyModerator_Codex_Persona_Context_V6`. That pack labels psychology/UX roles as working/simulated adaptations and disclaims market studies. No canonical project `MomentumUX` or `FieldFounder` source was found in the repository or supplied persona pack; A2/A3 provide independent bounded analyses, not claims of real merchant interviews.
8. **G1 adjudication:** Choose the task-first worklist over cosmetic compression because list-first removes non-actionable vertical cost while preserving contextual evidence. Keep no whole-queue card counts unless backed by a matching server query. On customers, use list + selected customer context on wide screens and sequential detail on mobile; RTO is evidence, not the 70% workspace. Defer segmentation, outreach, financial settlement views, person-level risk labels and unsupported bulk actions.
9. **Safety scope:** P0 review/patch before release: honor RTO contribution opt-out across both ingestion and network aggregation; make only final RTO outcomes count (not failed attempts) and deduplicate terminal webhook effects; do not auto-decline a buyer from a phone signal; prevent partially-paid orders from sending a full-total COD amount when the amount paid/due is unknown. Any change must have backend tests and an independent security retest. Do not expose hard order deletion; do not claim full customer erasure; constrain customer risk lookups to actual order/customer context; do not represent delivery or courier-reported COD as settled cash. Preserve the canonical owner/admin booking-confidence gate.
